# Synthetic Hackathon Data Licence and Use Notice

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Applies to:** Saab Industrial AI Hackathon — Synthetic Challenge Data Pack  
**Effective date:** 16 August 2026

## 1. Purpose

This synthetic data pack is provided solely to support participation in the Saab Industrial AI Hackathon and related demonstration, judging, mentoring and follow-up activities approved by the organisers.

The pack is intended for rapid prototype development, testing, learning and presentation. It is not intended for operational use, engineering release, procurement, certification, safety decisions, customer delivery or deployment in any production environment.

## 2. What is licensed

Subject to the conditions in this notice, the organisers grant each registered participant, mentor and judge a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable licence to:

- Download, copy and use the supplied synthetic data during the hackathon.
- Load the data into local tools, a team-owned database, Lovable, Supabase or another prototype environment.
- Transform, analyse, visualise and combine the data with team-created synthetic or openly licensed non-sensitive data.
- Create derivative prototypes, schemas, prompts, code, mockups, reports and demonstrations based on the data.
- Present the resulting prototype during the hackathon and related organiser-approved showcase activities.

This licence does **not** transfer ownership of the data pack or any associated Saab intellectual property.

## 3. Synthetic and fictional nature

All content in the data pack is fictional and notional, including but not limited to:

- Platforms, systems, components, compartments and configuration states.
- Asset names, roles, dependencies, locations, coordinates, relationships and network topology.
- Procedures, tests, safety notes, drawing descriptions, configuration rules and report templates.
- Health events, maintenance records, spares, stock quantities, lead times, capability indices, scenarios, actions and outcomes.
- Technical values, performance values, workflow conditions and decision logic.

The data does not represent real Saab, customer, supplier, government, military or operational information. It must not be interpreted as an authoritative source of technical, safety, maintenance, logistics, configuration, readiness, geographical or operational guidance.

## 4. Permitted use

Participants may use the pack to build a prototype that demonstrates, for example:

- Evidence-backed information retrieval and report drafting.
- Configuration applicability checks and workflow gates.
- Synthetic risk, dependency, logistics and resilience visualisation.
- What-if scenario exploration and transparent action trade-offs.
- Human-in-the-loop decision-support or workflow experiences.

Participants may add their own code, user interface, derived data, annotations and synthetic extensions. Any extension must remain clearly distinguishable from the canonical data pack and must retain the fictional/non-operational nature of the exercise.

## 5. Prohibited use

Participants must not:

- Use the pack for real-world operations, maintenance, engineering, safety, security, procurement, certification, training approval or decision-making.
- Treat a prototype result as a recommendation for a real platform, facility, supply chain, customer, government organisation or operational context.
- Add, upload, infer, reproduce, request or disclose classified, export-controlled, customer-specific, proprietary, personal, sensitive technical or operational data.
- Attempt to identify, reverse-engineer or associate fictional records with real facilities, platforms, personnel, product configurations, capabilities, locations, networks or procedures.
- Represent the data pack, derivative work or prototype as an official Saab product, capability, engineering artefact, standards implementation or validated operational model.
- Claim compliance, certification or conformance with S3000L, SAE GEIA-STD-0007, or any other standard based on use of this pack.
- Remove or obscure the fictionalisation, classification, licence or safety notices contained in the canonical files.
- Use the Saab name, logo, trademarks or other brand assets except as expressly approved by the organisers for the hackathon presentation.

## 6. Data handling and security

The supplied data is classified for the purposes of the hackathon as **Synthetic, non-classified, non-export-controlled**. This does not permit participants to mix it with sensitive information.

Participants must:

- Store the data only in their hackathon workspace, approved team repositories or team-owned prototype environments.
- Use access controls appropriate to the selected platform.
- Avoid placing credentials, API keys or personal information in public repositories, prompts, reports or demonstrations.
- Remove the supplied data from prototype environments after the event if requested by the organisers.
- Immediately notify the organisers if they suspect that sensitive or non-synthetic data has been introduced into the project.

## 7. AI services and generated output

Participants may use approved third-party AI, hosting, database and developer services at their own discretion and risk, subject to the organisers’ event rules and the service provider’s terms.

Before uploading any content to a third-party service, participants are responsible for confirming that the content remains limited to the approved synthetic pack and their own non-sensitive materials.

AI-generated outputs may be incorrect, incomplete or fabricated. Participants must ensure their prototype visibly distinguishes:

- Supplied evidence from generated text.
- Deterministic rule outcomes from AI-generated explanations.
- Known data from assumptions or hypotheses.
- Verified synthetic workflow state from missing-evidence or human-review-required state.

## 9. Public presentation

Teams may present their prototype at the hackathon and in organiser-approved follow-up events, provided that they:

- State that the prototype uses fictional, synthetic and non-operational data.
- Avoid claims that the prototype has been tested on real Saab or customer data.
- Do not imply endorsement, productisation, deployment or operational readiness unless separately confirmed in writing.
- Follow organiser guidance on screenshots, recordings, code sharing and social-media posts.

Suggested presentation disclaimer:

> This prototype uses fictional, synthetic and non-operational hackathon data. It is a demonstration of a possible workflow and is not a validated Saab product, operational system or technical decision tool.
